Why RolePebble

Job hunting is a second job. RolePebble does the paperwork.

Sending the same resume everywhere doesn't work, and rewriting it for every posting takes hours. RolePebble reads each posting, shows how you match, and writes a resume and cover letter for it from your own profile, so you spend your time on the jobs, not the documents.

Features

Everything between “I'm looking” and “I accept”

One place for your profile, your documents and your applications.

  • Tailored resume

    Written for each posting from your profile, in the tone you choose, and downloaded as a clean PDF.

  • Cover letter

    A letter for the role, not a template, with the important gaps addressed honestly.

  • See how you match

    Each requirement checked against your profile, with where it comes from. What's missing is shown as a gap.

  • Refine in a chat

    Ask for changes in plain words, edit any part yourself, and undo anything the AI changed.

  • Application board

    Every application on a board from draft to offer, with a dashboard of how your search is going.

  • Find jobs

    Search postings, score them against your profile, and start an application in one click.

  • Twelve resume themes

    From classic to modern, previewed live, so your resume looks right for the job.

  • 21 languages

    Resumes and cover letters in any of 21 languages, with headings and dates to match.

How it works

From a job posting to a sent application, in four steps

Set up your profile once. Every application after that takes minutes.

1/4

  1. Step 1

    Build your profile once

    Your experience, skills, projects and education in one place. Start from the resume you already have, as a PDF or Word file.

    • Import your existing resume
    • A strength meter shows what's missing

  2. Step 2

    Paste a job posting

    RolePebble pulls out what the job asks for and checks it against your profile, so you see where you stand before you apply.

    • Each requirement matched to your experience
    • Gaps shown, never papered over

  3. Step 3

    Get a tailored resume and cover letter

    Written for this posting from facts in your profile. Ask for changes in a chat until it's right, then download the PDFs.

    • Your tone, your theme, your language
    • Every version kept, every change undoable

  4. Step 4

    Apply and track it to an offer

    Every application on one board, from draft to sent, interview and offer, with a dashboard that shows what to follow up next.

    • Board and table of every application
    • See what's working, week by week

Honest by design

It never invents experience.

Drafts only use facts from your profile. When a posting asks for something you don't have, RolePebble shows you the gap instead of making something up, and you decide what to do about it.

  • Nothing is sent or applied for without you
  • Gaps you fill go into your profile for next time
  • Export all your data, or delete it, any time

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Write to support@rolepebble.com.

When can I use it?

RolePebble is in development. People on the waitlist get invited to the private beta first, in small groups, and we'll email you when it's your turn.

How much will it cost?

There'll be a free plan to get started, and paid plans for more tailored applications. Prices will be announced before launch. The private beta is free.

Will it make things up to get me the job?

No. Resumes and cover letters only use facts from your profile. When a job asks for something you don't have, you'll see it as a gap, not as an invented line on your resume.

What happens to my data?

Your profile and documents are only used to write your applications. You can export everything as one file or delete it all at any time. The privacy policy says exactly who receives what.

Who's behind RolePebble?

RolePebble is run by Ahmad Usama. It's built on Dossier, an open-source job search app by AienTech, and like Dossier its source code is public under the GNU AGPL.
